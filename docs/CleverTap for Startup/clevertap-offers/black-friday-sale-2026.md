# Overview

The CleverTap Black Friday Sale 2026 gives new customers a choice of two discounts on the CleverTap Core plan. This document explains both offers and their terms and conditions.

# Offers

When you sign up for the CleverTap Core plan through a Black Friday Sale link, you can choose one of the following two offers:

| Offer   | Discount | Discount period                                  |
| ------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Offer 1 | 75% off  | 12 months, starting after your 30-day free trial |
| Offer 2 | 50% off  | 6 months, starting after your 30-day free trial  |

You choose an offer by using its signup link. The offer is applied to your subscription automatically when your account is created, so you do not need to enter a coupon code.

## Terms and Conditions

The following are the terms and conditions to avail the Black Friday Sale 2026 offer:

- The sale runs from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- The offers are available only to new CleverTap customers.
- The offers apply to all monthly active user (MAU) tiers on the CleverTap Core plan monthly subscription.
- You can choose only one offer. These two offers cannot be combined.
- The offers can not be combined with any other promotion, coupon, or credit.
- The discount applies to your CleverTap Core plan and to add-ons, except the WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) and Email BSP add-ons. These two add-ons are excluded from the discount.
- The discount starts after your 30-day free trial ends.
- When the discount period ends, your subscription continues at the full CleverTap Core plan price.
- Taxes apply in addition to the discounted amount.
- The offers apply only when you sign up through a Black Friday Sale signup link. Signups made after the sale ends do not receive the discount.

# FAQs

The following are the frequently asked questions you may have regarding the CleverTap Black Friday Sale 2026:

### Q. How does the discount appear in your account?

During signup, you can see the applied offer and the discounted price for each MAU tier. On the payments page, you can see the amount you pay during the discount period and the full CleverTap Core plan price that applies after the discount period ends.

After your account is created, the discount appears on the _My Plan_ page.

### Q. Which offer should I choose?

Choose the offer that suits your plan. Offer 1 gives a higher discount (75%) for a longer period (12 months). Offer 2 gives a lower discount (50%) for a shorter period (6 months).

### Q. Can I get the offers if I am an existing customer?

No. The Black Friday Sale 2026 offers are available to new customers only.

### Q. Can I use the offer with another coupon or credit?

No. You can not combine an offer with any other promotion, coupon, or credit.

### Q. Does the offer apply to add-ons?

The discount applies to add-ons except the WhatsApp BSP and Email BSP.

### Q. When does the discount start?

The discount starts after your 30-day free trial ends.

### Q. What happens when the discount ends?

After the discount period ends, your subscription continues at the full Core plan price.

### Q. What if I sign up after the sale ends?

If you sign up after the sale ends, your account will be created without the offer, and the standard Core plan price will apply.
