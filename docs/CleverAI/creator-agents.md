# Overview

Creator Agents generate on-brand, contextual content in seconds, such as copy, images, email templates, and WhatsApp templates. Describe what you want in a prompt, and the agent produces a first draft you can refine or use as-is. Scribe drafts copy informed by your past successful campaigns, Image Creator generates visuals, and the Template Creator agents build ready-to-edit Email and WhatsApp templates.

As CleverAI evolves, the content these agents generate feeds directly into CleverTap's decisioning engine, so your content options and your optimization layer increasingly draw from the same pool, rather than working as two disconnected steps.
